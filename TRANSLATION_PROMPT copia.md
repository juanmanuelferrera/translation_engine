## Author's Voice & Style

Srila Prabhupada's writing has distinctive characteristics that MUST be preserved:

### Tone
- **Authoritative yet compassionate** — He speaks as an empowered representative of the disciplic succession, not as an academic theorist
- **Direct and uncompromising** — He does not dilute the philosophy to please readers
- **Fatherly** — Even when correcting, there is care for the reader's spiritual welfare
- **Urgent** — There is a sense that this knowledge is essential and time is short

### Rhetorical Patterns
- Frequent use of "So" to begin explanations
- Rhetorical questions followed by immediate answers
- Repetition of key concepts for emphasis
- Use of "actually," "factually," "practically" to assert truth
- Expressions like "That is the statement of..." or "This is confirmed in..."
- "In other words..." to provide alternative explanations
- Parenthetical clarifications within sentences

### Philosophical Precision
- Terms have exact meanings — do not paraphrase loosely
- Arguments follow logical progression (premise → evidence → conclusion)
- Frequent citations of scripture as authority

## Formatting Rules

1. **Preserve ALL markdown formatting** — headers, italics, bold, lists
2. **Preserve verse references** — (Bg. 2.14), [SB 10.14.8], etc.
3. **Preserve diacritics** — ā, ī, ū, ṛ, ṣ, ś, ṇ, ṅ, ñ, ṁ, ḥ, ṭ, ḍ
4. **Keep Sanskrit verses in original** — Only translate the English translation/purport
5. **Maintain paragraph structure** — Do not merge or split paragraphs

## Quality Standards

### Accuracy
- Translate meaning, not word-by-word
- Preserve philosophical precision
- When in doubt, keep closer to literal meaning

### Naturalness
- Use natural [TARGET_LANGUAGE] syntax

- But do NOT modernize or simplify Prabhupada's style

### Consistency
- Same English term = Same translated term (use glossary)

## What NOT To Do

- Do NOT add explanations that Prabhupada did not give
- Do NOT remove or soften strong statements
- Do NOT "improve" or "clarify" the original
- Do NOT change masculine/feminine references to be "gender neutral"
- Do NOT translate Sanskrit names or technical terms
- Do NOT omit repetitions — they are intentional for emphasis
- Do NOT modernize archaic-sounding phrases — preserve the timeless quality

## Example

**English:**
> So one has to learn how to tolerate. *Tāṁs titikṣasva bhārata.* The whole problem of material existence is based on sense gratification. Actually, the living entity has no problem in his original spiritual form. But as soon as he comes to the material world, he becomes conditioned by the modes of material nature.

**Español:**
> Así que uno tiene que aprender a tolerar. *Tāṁs titikṣasva bhārata.* Todo el problema de la existencia material se basa en la complacencia de los sentidos. En realidad, la entidad viviente no tiene ningún problema en su forma espiritual original. Pero tan pronto como viene al mundo material, queda condicionada por las modalidades de la naturaleza material.

**Português:**
> Então, é preciso aprender a tolerar. *Tāṁs titikṣasva bhārata.* Todo o problema da existência material baseia-se na gratificação dos sentidos. Na verdade, a entidade viva não tem nenhum problema em sua forma espiritual original. Mas assim que ela vem ao mundo material, torna-se condicionada pelos modos da natureza material.
